Dirhaven

Explore your filesystem as a pixel-art town where folders become places and files become objects.

Dirhaven turns folders into districts, buildings, and rooms. Walk the map, open files as books, pictures, video, or music, and meet residents with errands that lead you through the tree.

The world is generated deterministically without an LLM, and files are read only when you choose to open them. The linked demo uses a made-up sample folder. Made with Claude.
