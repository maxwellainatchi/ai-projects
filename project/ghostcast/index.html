Ghostcast

Go live to an AI chat audience that reacts to your voice, camera, and stream moments.

Ghostcast is a pretend livestream: turn on your camera and mic, then watch an AI-written audience react to what you say and show. Nothing is broadcast to other people.

The chat has its own rhythm, emotes, and reactions to stream events, so it feels lively even when you pause.
